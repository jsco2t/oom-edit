## Measured reference

The final 5×20 recorder produced 7,200 samples across 20 cases, two sizes, and 72 case/step/size groups. Each sample carries fixture hash, trial, cycle, input, frame, total, and process peak RSS. At 1 MiB mixed Markdown, source-line insertion was 79.7 ms median / 217.4 ms p99 and deletion 60.8 / 123.1 ms. Rendered line deletion was 639.9 / 663.4 ms at top and 647.6 / 670.7 ms at midpoint; undo at top was 523.0 / 537.8 ms. Source and rendered scroll were 16.3 / 16.5 ms and 1.0 / 1.1 ms respectively; return from Insert was 207.6 / 258.2 ms. An explicit `rust`→`unknown` fence-label transition was 485.5 / 1,114.6 ms, measured as one multi-key operation. Unchanged save was 4.5 / 4.6 ms. Rendered delete/undo peaked at 234.3 MiB RSS, resize at 243.2 MiB, and reload at 200.5 MiB.

The final five-trial cold reference measured 1 MiB mixed first frame at 558.8 ms median / 565.8 ms worst (100×41) and 559.1 / 561.9 ms (70×28). Its full 1 MiB mixed layout retained 50.9 MiB and reached 179.5 MiB process RSS; the public first-frame process reached 182.6 MiB. The 1 MiB edit and resize paths can exceed 192 MiB RSS because old and new complete layouts coexist transiently; this is recorded rather than concealed.
